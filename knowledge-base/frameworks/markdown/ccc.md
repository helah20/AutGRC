# Cloud Cybersecurity Controls (CCC-2:2024)

Publisher: National Cybersecurity Authority (NCA), Saudi Arabia. Source file: CCC-2-2024-EN-.pdf.

Each control is addressed to the cloud service provider (P, CSP) or the cloud service tenant (T, CST): 37 + 18 main controls and 94 + 26 subcontrols.

## CCC domain 1: Cybersecurity Governance

### CCC 1-1 Cybersecurity Roles and Responsibilities

Objective: To ensure that the roles and responsibilities are defined for all parties participating in implementing the cloud cybersecurity controls, including the roles and responsibilities of the head of the CSP and CST or his/her delegate, referred to in these controls as the “Authorizing Official.”

- **CCC 1-1-P-1**: In addition to the ECC control 1-4-1, the Authorizing Official shall also identify, document, and approve: [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 1-1-P-1-1**: Cybersecurity roles and RACI assignment for all stakeholders of the cloud services including the Authorizing Official's roles and responsibilities. [applies to CSP]
- **CCC 1-1-T-1**: In addition to the ECC control 1-4-1, the Authorizing Official shall also identify, document, and approve: [applies to CST]
  - **CCC 1-1-T-1-1**: Cybersecurity roles and RACI assignment for all stakeholders of the cloud services including the Authorizing Official's roles and responsibilities. [applies to CST]

### CCC 1-2 Cybersecurity Risk Management

Objective: To ensure managing cybersecurity risks in a methodological approach in order to protect the CSP’s and CST’s information and technology assets as per entity-related policies and procedures, and related laws and regulations.

- **CCC 1-2-P-1**: Cybersecurity risk management methodology mentioned in the ECC Subdomain 1-5, shall also include for the CSP, as a minimum: [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 1-2-P-1-1**: Defining acceptable risk levels for the cloud services and clarifying them to the CST if they are related to the CST. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 1-2-P-1-2**: Considering data and information classification in cybersecurity risk management methodology. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 1-2-P-1-3**: Developing cybersecurity risk register for cloud services and monitoring it periodically according to the risks. [applies to CSP]
- **CCC 1-2-T-1**: Cybersecurity risk management methodology mentioned in the ECC Subdomain 1-5 shall also include for the CST, as a minimum: [applies to CST]
  - **CCC 1-2-T-1-1**: Defining acceptable risk levels for the cloud services. [applies to CST]
  - **CCC 1-2-T-1-2**: Considering data and information classification accredited by CST in cybersecurity risk management methodology. [applies to CST]
  - **CCC 1-2-T-1-3**: Developing cybersecurity risk register for cloud services and monitoring it periodically according to the risks. [applies to CST]

### CCC 1-3 Compliance with Cybersecurity Standards, Laws, and Regulations

Objective: To ensure that the CSPs’ and CSTs’ cybersecurity program is in compliance with related laws and regulations.

- **CCC 1-3-P-1**: In addition to the ECC control 1-7-1, the CSP legislative and regulatory compliance should include as a minimum with the following requirements: [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 1-3-P-1-1**: Continuous compliance with all laws, regulations, instructions, decisions, regulatory frameworks and controls, and mandates regarding cybersecurity in the Kingdom [applies to CSP]
- **CCC 1-3-T-1**: In addition to the ECC control 1-7-1, the CST legislative and regulatory compliance should include as a minimum with the following requirements: [applies to CST]
  - **CCC 1-3-T-1-1**: Continuous or real-time compliance monitoring of the CSP with relevant cybersecurity legislation and contract clauses. [applies to CST]

### CCC 1-4 Cybersecurity in Human Resources

Objective: To ensure that cybersecurity risks and requirements related to personnel (employees and contractors) are managed efficiently prior to employment, during employment and after termination/separation as per entity-related policies and procedures, and related laws and regulations.

- **CCC 1-4-P-1**: In addition to Subcontrols in the ECC controls 1-9-3 and 1-9-4, the following requirements should be covered prior and during the professional relationship of personnel with the CSP as a minimum: [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 1-4-P-1-1**: Positions of cybersecurity functions in CSP’s data centers within the Kingdom must be filled with qualified and suitable Saudi nationals. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 1-4-P-1-2**: Screening or vetting candidates of personnel working inside the Kingdom who have access to Cloud Technology Stack, periodically. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 1-4-P-1-3**: Cybersecurity policies as a prerequisite to access to Cloud Technology Stack, signed and appropriately approved. [applies to CSP]
- **CCC 1-4-P-2**: In addition to Subcontrols in the ECC control 1-9-5, the following requirements should be in place, as a minimum, for the termination/completion of a human resource’s professional relationship with the CSP: [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 1-4-P-2-1**: Assurance that assets owned by the entity (especially those with security exposure) are accounted for and returned upon termination. [applies to CSP]
- **CCC 1-4-T-1**: In addition to Subcontrols in the ECC control 1-9-3, the following requirements should be covered prior the professional relationship of staff with the CST shall cover, at a minimum: [applies to CST]
  - **CCC 1-4-T-1-1**: Screening or vetting candidates of personnel with access to Cloud Service sensitive functions (Key Management, Service Administration, Access Control). [applies to CST]

### CCC 1-5 Cybersecurity in Change Management

Objective: To ensure that cybersecurity requirements are included in change management methodology and procedures in order to protect the confidentiality, integrity and availability of information and technology assets as per CSPs policies and procedures, and related laws and regulations.

- **CCC 1-5-P-1**: Cybersecurity requirements for change management within the CSP shall be identified, documented, and approved. [applies to CSP]
- **CCC 1-5-P-2**: Cybersecurity requirements for change management within the CSP shall be applied [applies to CSP]
- **CCC 1-5-P-3**: Cybersecurity for change management in the CSP shall cover, as a minimum: [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 1-5-P-3-1**: Processes and procedures to securely implement changes (planned works) in production systems, with priority given to cybersecurity observations. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 1-5-P-3-2**: Process for the implementation of cybersecurity exceptional changes (e.g.: changes during incident restoration). [applies to CSP]
- **CCC 1-5-P-4**: Cybersecurity requirements for change management within the CSP shall be applied and reviewed periodically. [applies to CSP]

## CCC domain 2: Cybersecurity Defense

### CCC 2-1 Asset Management

Objective: To ensure that the CSP and CST has an accurate and detailed inventory of information and technology assets in order to support the entity cybersecurity and operational requirements to maintain the confidentiality, integrity and availability of information and technology assets.

- **CCC 2-1-P-1**: In addition to controls in the ECC control 2-1, the CSP shall cover the following additional controls for cybersecurity requirements for cybersecurity event logs and monitoring management, as a minimum: [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-1-P-1-1**: Inventory of all information and technology assets using suitable techniques such as Configuration Management Database (CMDB) or similar capability containing an inventory of all technical assets. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-1-P-1-2**: Identifying assets owners and involving them in the asset management lifecycle. [applies to CSP]
- **CCC 2-1-T-1**: In addition to controls in the ECC control 2-1, the CST shall cover the following additional controls for cybersecurity requirements for cybersecurity event logs and monitoring management, as a minimum: [applies to CST]
  - **CCC 2-1-T-1-1**: Inventory of all cloud services and information and technology assets related to the cloud services. [applies to CST]

### CCC 2-2 Identity and Access Management

Objective: To ensure the secure and restricted logical access to information and technology assets in order to prevent unauthorized access and allow only authorized access for users which are necessary to accomplish assigned tasks.

- **CCC 2-2-P-1**: In addition to Subcontrols in the ECC control 2-2-3, the CSP shall cover the following additional Subcontrols for cybersecurity requirements for identity and access management requirements, as a minimum: [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-2-P-1-1**: Identity and access management of generic accounts credentials for accountability cannot be assigned for a specific individual. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-2-P-1-2**: Secure session management, including session authenticity, session lockout, and session timeout termination. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-2-P-1-3**: Multi-factor authentication for privileged users, and candidates of personnel with access to Cloud Technology Stack. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-2-P-1-4**: Formal process to detect and prevent unauthorized access (e.g., unsuccessful login attempt threshold). [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-2-P-1-5**: Utilizing secure methods and algorithms for saving and processing passwords, such as: Secure Hashing functions. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-2-P-1-6**: Secure management of thirdparty personnel’s accounts. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-2-P-1-7**: Access control enforced to management systems, administrative consoles. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-2-P-1-8**: Masking of displayed authentication inputs, especially passwords, to prevent shoulder surfing. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-2-P-1-9**: Getting CST’s approval before accessing any CST-related asset by the CSP or CSP’s third parties. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-2-P-1-10**: Capability to immediately interrupt a remote access session and prevent any future access for a user. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-2-P-1-11**: Provision to CSTs of multi-factor authentication services for privileged cloud users. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-2-P-1-12**: Assurance of restricted and controlled access to storage systems and means (such as Storage Area Network (SAN)). [applies to CSP]
- **CCC 2-2-T-1**: In addition to Subcontrols in the ECC control 2-2-3, the CST shall cover the following additional Subcontrols for cybersecurity requirements for identity and access management requirements, as a minimum: [applies to CST]
  - **CCC 2-2-T-1-1**: Identity and access management for all cloud credentials along their full lifecycle. [applies to CST]
  - **CCC 2-2-T-1-2**: Confidentiality of cloud user identification, cloud credential and cloud access rights information, including the requirement on users to keep them private (for employed, third party and CST personnel). [applies to CST]
  - **CCC 2-2-T-1-3**: Secure session management, including session authenticity, session lockout, and session timeout termination on the cloud. [applies to CST]
  - **CCC 2-2-T-1-4**: Multi-factor authentication for privileged cloud users. [applies to CST]
  - **CCC 2-2-T-1-5**: Formal process to detect and prevent unauthorized access to cloud (such as a threshold of unsuccessful login attempts). [applies to CST]

### CCC 2-3 Information System and Information Processing Facilities Protection

Objective: To ensure the protection of information systems and information processing facilities (including workstations and infrastructures) against cyber risks.

- **CCC 2-3-P-1**: In addition to Subcontrols in the ECC control 2-3-3, the CSP shall cover the following additional Subcontrols for cybersecurity requirements for information system and processing facilities protection requirements, as a minimum: [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-3-P-1-1**: Ensuring that all configurations are applied in accordance with CSP’s cybersecurity standards. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-3-P-1-2**: Assurance of separation and isolation of data, environments, and information systems across CSTs, to prevent data commingling. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-3-P-1-3**: Adopting cybersecurity principles for technical system configurations adhering to the minimum functionality principle. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-3-P-1-4**: Ability of the Cloud Technology Stacks to securely handle input validation, exceptions and failure. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-3-P-1-5**: Full isolation of security functions and applications from other functions and applications in the Cloud Technology Stack. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-3-P-1-6**: Notification to CSTs with cybersecurity requirements provided by the CSP that are useable by the CST. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-3-P-1-7**: Detection and prevention of unauthorized changes to software programs, and systems. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-3-P-1-8**: Complete isolation and protection of multiple guest environments. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-3-P-1-9**: The community cloud services provided to CSTs (government entities and CNI entities) shall be isolated from any other cloud computing provided to entities outside the scope of work. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-3-P-1-10**: Modern technologies, such as Endpoint Detection and Response (EDR) technologies, to ensure that the information servers and devices of CSP’s information processing systems and devices of are ready for rapid response to incidents. [applies to CSP]
- **CCC 2-3-T-1**: In addition to Subcontrols in the ECC control 2-3-3, the CST shall cover the following additional Subcontrols for cybersecurity requirements for information system and processing facilities protection requirements, as a minimum: [applies to CST]
  - **CCC 2-3-T-1-1**: Verifying that the CSP isolates the community cloud services provided to CSTs (government entities and CNI entities) from any other cloud computing provided to entities outside the scope of work. [applies to CST]

### CCC 2-4 Networks Security Management

Objective: To ensure the protection of CSP’s and CST’s network from cyber risks.

- **CCC 2-4-P-1**: In addition to Subcontrols in the ECC control 2-5-3, the CSP shall cover the following additional Subcontrols for cybersecurity requirements for networks security management requirements, as a minimum: [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-4-P-1-1**: Monitoring of traffic across the external and internal networks to detect anomalies. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-4-P-1-2**: Network isolation and protection of Cloud Technology Stack network from other internal and external networks. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-4-P-1-3**: Protection from denial-of-service attacks (including Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS)). [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-4-P-1-4**: Protection of data transmitted through the network; from and to the Cloud Technology Stack network using cryptography primitives; for management and administrative access. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-4-P-1-5**: Access control between different network segments. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-4-P-1-6**: Isolation between cloud service delivery network, cloud management network and CSP enterprise network. [applies to CSP]
- **CCC 2-4-T-1**: In addition to Subcontrols in the ECC control 2-5-3, the CST shall cover the following additional Subcontrols for cybersecurity requirements for networks security management requirements, as a minimum: [applies to CST]
  - **CCC 2-4-T-1-1**: Protecting the connection channel with CSP. [applies to CST]

### CCC 2-5 Mobile Devices Security

Objective: To ensure the protection of mobile devices (including laptops, smartphones, and tablets) from cyber risks and to ensure the secure handling of the CSPs’ and CSTs’ information (including sensitive information) while utilizing mobile devices.

- **CCC 2-5-P-1**: In addition to Subcontrols in the ECC control 2-6-3, the CSP shall cover the following additional Subcontrols for cybersecurity requirements for mobile device security, as a minimum: [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-5-P-1-1**: Inventory of all end user and mobile devices. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-5-P-1-2**: Centralized mobile device security management. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-5-P-1-3**: Screen looking for end user devices. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-5-P-1-4**: Data sanitation and secure disposal for end-user devices, especially for those with exposure to the Cloud Technology Stack. [applies to CSP]
- **CCC 2-5-T-1**: In addition to Subcontrols in the ECC control 2-6-3, the CST shall cover the following additional Subcontrols for cybersecurity requirements for mobile device security, as a minimum: [applies to CST]
  - **CCC 2-5-T-1-1**: Data sanitation and secure disposal for end-user devices with access to the cloud services. [applies to CST]

### CCC 2-6 Data and Information Protection

Objective: To ensure the confidentiality, integrity and availability of CSPs’ and CSTs’ data and information as per entity-related policies and procedures, and related laws and regulations.

- **CCC 2-6-P-1**: In addition to Subcontrols in the ECC control 2-7-3, the CSP shall cover the following additional Subcontrols for cybersecurity requirements for data and information protection requirements, as a minimum: [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-6-P-1-1**: Prohibiting the use of Cloud Technology Stack’s data in any environment other than production environment, except after applying strict controls for protecting that data, such as data masking or data scrambling techniques. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-6-P-1-2**: Provision to CSTs of securely data storage processes, procedures, and technologies to comply with related legal and regulatory requirements. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-6-P-1-3**: Disposal of CST’s data should be performed in a secure manner on termination or expiry of the contract with the CSP. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-6-P-1-4**: Commitment to maintain the confidentiality of the CST’s data and information, according to related legal and regulatory requirements. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-6-P-1-5**: Providing CSTs with secure means to export and transfer data and virtual infrastructure. [applies to CSP]
- **CCC 2-6-T-1**: In addition to Subcontrols in the ECC control 2-7-3, the CST shall cover the following additional Subcontrols for cybersecurity requirements for protecting CST’s data and information in cloud computing, as a minimum: [applies to CST]
  - **CCC 2-6-T-1-1**: Exit Strategy to ensure means for secure disposal of data on termination or expiry of the contract with the CSP. [applies to CST]
  - **CCC 2-6-T-1-2**: Using secure means to export and transfer data and virtual infrastructure. [applies to CST]

### CCC 2-7 Cryptography

Objective: To ensure the proper and efficient use of cryptography to protect information assets as per policies, procedures, and related laws and regulations.

- **CCC 2-7-P-1**: In addition to Subcontrols in the ECC control 2-8-3, the CSP shall cover the following additional Subcontrols for cryptography, as a minimum: [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-7-P-1-1**: Technical mechanisms and cryptographic primitives for strong encryption, in according to the advanced level in the National Cryptographic Standards (NCS-1:2020). [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-7-P-1-2**: Certification authority and issuance capability in a secure manner, or usage of certificates from a trusted certification authority. [applies to CSP]
- **CCC 2-7-T-1**: In addition to subcontrols in the ECC control 2-8-3, the CST shall cover the following additional subcontrols for cryptography, as a minimum: [applies to CST]
  - **CCC 2-7-T-1-1**: Technical mechanisms and cryptographic primitives for strong encryption, in according to the advanced level in the National Cryptographic Standards (NCS-1:2020). [applies to CST]
  - **CCC 2-7-T-1-2**: Encryption of data and information transferred to or transferred out of the cloud according to the relevant law and regulatory requirements. [applies to CST]

### CCC 2-8 Backup and Recovery Management

Objective: To ensure the protection of CSPs’ data and information including information systems and software configurations from cyber risks as per entity-related policies and procedures, and related laws and regulations.

- **CCC 2-8-P-1**: In addition to subcontrols in the ECC control 2-9-3, the CSP shall cover the following additional subcontrols for cybersecurity requirements for backup and recovery management, as a minimum: [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-8-P-1-1**: Securing access, storage and transfer of CST’s data backups and its mediums, and protecting it against damage, amendment, or unauthorized access. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-8-P-1-2**: Securing access, storage and transfer of Cloud Technology Stack backups and its mediums, and protecting it against damage, amendment, or unauthorized access. [applies to CSP]

### CCC 2-9 Vulnerabilities Management

Objective: To ensure timely detection and effective remediation of technical vulnerabilities to prevent or minimize the probability of exploiting these vulnerabilities to launch cyber attacks against the CSP and CST.

- **CCC 2-9-P-1**: In addition to subcontrols in the ECC control 2-10-3, the CSP shall cover the following additional subcontrols for cybersecurity requirements for vulnerability management requirements, as a minimum: [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-9-P-1-1**: Assessing and remediating vulnerabilities on external components of Cloud Technology Stack at least once every month, and at least once every three months for internal components of Cloud Technology Stack. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-9-P-1-2**: Notification to CSTs of identified vulnerabilities that may affecting them, and safeguards in place. [applies to CSP]
- **CCC 2-9-T-1**: In addition to subcontrols in the ECC control 2-10-3, the CST shall cover the following additional subcontrols for cybersecurity requirements for vulnerability management requirements, as a minimum: [applies to CST]
  - **CCC 2-9-T-1-1**: Assessing and remediating vulnerabilities cloud services and at least once every three months. [applies to CST]
  - **CCC 2-9-T-1-2**: Management of CSP-notified vulnerabilities safeguards in place. [applies to CST]

### CCC 2-10 Penetration Testing

Objective: To assess and evaluate the efficiency of the CSP’s cybersecurity defense capabilities through simulated cyber-attacks to discover unknown weaknesses within the technical infrastructure that may lead to a cyber breach.

- **CCC 2-10-P-1**: In addition to subcontrols in the ECC control 2-11-3, the CSP shall cover the following additional subcontrols for cybersecurity requirements for penetration testing, as a minimum: [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-10-P-1-1**: Scope of penetration tests must cover Cloud Technology Stack and must be conducted at least once every six months. [applies to CSP]

### CCC 2-11 Cybersecurity Event Logs and Monitoring Management

Objective: Ensure timely collection, analysis, and monitoring of cybersecurity event logs for the proactive detection and effective management of cyber-attacks to prevent or minimize the impact on the CSPs’ and CSTs’ business.

- **CCC 2-11-P-1**: In addition to subcontrols in the ECC control 2-12-3, the CSP shall cover the following additional subcontrols for cybersecurity requirements for cybersecurity event logs and monitoring management, as a minimum: [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-11-P-1-1**: Activating and protecting event logs and audit trails of Cloud Technology Stack. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-11-P-1-2**: Activating and collecting login attempts history. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-11-P-1-3**: Activating and protecting all event logs of activities and operations performed by the CSP at the tenant level in order to support forensic analysis. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-11-P-1-4**: Protecting cybersecurity event logs from alteration, disclosure, destruction and unauthorized access and unauthorized release, in accordance with regulatory, or law requirements. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-11-P-1-5**: Continuous cybersecurity events monitoring using SIEM technique covering the full Cloud Technology Stack. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-11-P-1-6**: Reviewing cybersecurity event logs and audit trails periodically, covering CSP events in the Cloud Technology Stack. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-11-P-1-7**: Automated monitoring and logging of remote access sessions event logs. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-11-P-1-8**: Secure handling of user-related data found in the audit trails and the cybersecurity event logs. [applies to CSP]
- **CCC 2-11-T-1**: In addition to subcontrols in the ECC control 2-12-3, the CST shall cover the following additional subcontrols for cybersecurity requirements for cybersecurity event logs and monitoring management, as a minimum: [applies to CST]
  - **CCC 2-11-T-1-1**: Activating and collecting of login event logs, and cybersecurity event logs on assets related to cloud services. [applies to CST]
  - **CCC 2-11-T-1-2**: Monitoring shall include all activated cybersecurity logs on the cloud services of the CST. [applies to CST]

### CCC 2-12 Cybersecurity Incident and Threat Management

Objective: Ensure timely identification and detection of cybersecurity incidents and their effective management and proactive response to cybersecurity threats to prevent or minimize the impact of the impacts resulting on the business of the CSPs.

- **CCC 2-12-P-1**: In addition to subcontrols in the ECC control 2-13-3, the CSP shall cover the following additional subcontrols for cybersecurity requirements for cybersecurity incident and threat management, as a minimum: [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-12-P-1-1**: Subscribing in authorized and specialized entities and groups to stay upto-date on Cybersecurity threats, common practices, and key knowhow. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-12-P-1-2**: Training for employees and third-party personnel to respond to cybersecurity incidents, in line with their roles and responsibilities. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-12-P-1-3**: Periodically testing the incident response capability. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-12-P-1-4**: Root Cause Analysis of cybersecurity incidents and developing plans to address them. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-12-P-1-5**: Support the CST in cases legal proceedings and forensics, protecting the chain of custody that falls under the management and responsibility of the CSP, in accordance with the related law and regulatory requirements. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-12-P-1-6**: Real-time reporting to the CST of incidents that may affect CST; if the incident is discovered. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-12-P-1-7**: Support for CSTs to handle security incidents according to the agreement between the CSP and CST. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-12-P-1-8**: Measuring and monitoring cybersecurity incident metrics and monitor compliance with contracts and legislative requirements. [applies to CSP]

### CCC 2-13 Physical Security

Objective: To ensure the protection of CSPs’ information and technology assets from unauthorized physical access, loss, theft, and damage.

- **CCC 2-13-P-1**: In addition to subcontrols in the ECC control 2-14-3, the CSP shall cover the following additional subcontrols for cybersecurity requirements for physical security, as a minimum: [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-13-P-1-1**: Continual monitoring of access to CSP’s sites and buildings. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-13-P-1-2**: Preventing unauthorized access to devices in the Cloud Technology Stack. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-13-P-1-3**: Disposal of cloud infrastructure hardware, in particular, storage equipment (external or internal), by adopting relevant legislation and best practices. [applies to CSP]

### CCC 2-14 Web Application Security

Objective: Ensure the protection of external web applications of the CSP from cyber risks.

- **CCC 2-14-P-1**: In addition to subcontrols in the ECC control 2-15-3, the CSP shall cover the following additional subcontrols for cybersecurity requirements for web application security, as a minimum: [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-14-P-1-1**: Protecting information involved in application service transactions against possible risks (e.g.: incomplete transmission, mis-routing, unauthorized message alteration, unauthorized disclosure….). [applies to CSP]

### CCC 2-15 Key Management

Objective: Ensure secure management of CSPs’ and CSTs’ cryptographic keys to protect confidentiality, integrity and availability of information and technical assets.

- **CCC 2-15-P-1**: Cybersecurity requirements for key management process within the CSP shall be identified, documented, and approved. [applies to CSP]
- **CCC 2-15-P-2**: Cybersecurity requirements for key management process within the CSP shall be applied. [applies to CSP]
- **CCC 2-15-P-3**: In addition to the ECC Subcontrol 2-8-3-2, cybersecurity requirements for key management within the CSP shall cover, at minimum, the following: [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-15-P-3-1**: Ensure well-defined ownership for cryptographic keys. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-15-P-3-2**: A secure cryptographic key retrieval mechanism in case of cryptographic key lost (such as backup of keys and enforcement of trusted key storage, strictly external to cloud). [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-15-P-3-3**: Activating and monitoring of all audit trails of keys. [applies to CSP]
- **CCC 2-15-P-4**: Cybersecurity requirements for key management within the CSP shall be applied and reviewed periodically. [applies to CSP]
- **CCC 2-15-T-1**: Cybersecurity requirements for key management within the CST shall be identified, documented, and approved. [applies to CST]
- **CCC 2-15-T-2**: Cybersecurity requirements for key management within the CST shall applied. [applies to CST]
- **CCC 2-15-T-3**: In addition to the ECC Subcontrol 2-8-3-2, cybersecurity requirements for key management within the CST shall cover, at minimum, the following: [applies to CST]
  - **CCC 2-15-T-3-1**: Ensure well-defined ownership for cryptographic keys. [applies to CST]
  - **CCC 2-15-T-3-2**: A secure data retrieval mechanism in case of cryptographic encryption key lost (such as backup of keys and enforcement of trusted key storage, strictly external to cloud). [applies to CST]
- **CCC 2-15-T-4**: Cybersecurity requirements for key management within the CST shall be applied and reviewed periodically. [applies to CST]

### CCC 2-16 System Development Security

Objective: Ensure CSPs’ systems are developed, integrated, and deployed in a secure manner.

- **CCC 2-16-P-1**: Cybersecurity requirements for system development within the CSP shall be identified, documented, and approved. [applies to CSP]
- **CCC 2-16-P-2**: Cybersecurity requirements for system development within the CSP shall be applied. [applies to CSP]
- **CCC 2-16-P-3**: Cybersecurity requirements for system development within the CSP shall include as a minimum the following controls along the development lifecycle: [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-16-P-3-1**: Considering cybersecurity requirements of the Cloud Technology Stack and relevant systems in the design and implementation of the cloud computing services. [applies to CSP]

### CCC 2-17 Storage Media Security

Objective: Ensure CSPs’ secure handling of information and data on physical media.

- **CCC 2-17-P-1**: Cybersecurity requirements for usage of information and data media within the CSP shall be identified, documented, and approved. [applies to CSP]
- **CCC 2-17-P-2**: Cybersecurity requirements for usage of information and data media within the CSP shall be applied. [applies to CSP]
- **CCC 2-17-P-3**: Cybersecurity requirements for usage of information and data media within the CSP shall cover, at minimum, the following: [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-17-P-3-1**: Enforcement of sanitization of media, prior to disposal or reuse. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-17-P-3-2**: Using secure means when disposing of media. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-17-P-3-3**: Provision to maintain confidentiality and integrity of data on removable media. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-17-P-3-4**: Human readable labelling of media, to explain its classification and the sensitivity of the information it contains. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 2-17-P-3-5**: Controlled and physically secure storage of removable media. [applies to CSP]

## CCC domain 3: Cybersecurity Resilience

### CCC 3-1 Cybersecurity Resilience Aspects of Business Continuity Management (BCM)

Objective: To ensure the inclusion of the cybersecurity resiliency requirements within the CSPs’ and CSTs’ business continuity management and to remediate and minimize the impacts on systems, information processing facilities and critical e-services from disasters caused by cybersecurity incidents.

- **CCC 3-1-P-1**: In addition to subcontrols in the ECC control 3-1-3, the CSP shall cover the following additional subcontrols for cybersecurity requirements for cybersecurity resilience aspects of business continuity management, as a minimum: [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 3-1-P-1-1**: Developing and implementing disaster recovery and business continuity procedures in a secure manner. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 3-1-P-1-2**: Developing and implementing procedures to ensure resilience and continuity of cybersecurity systems dedicated to the protection of Cloud Technology Stack. [applies to CSP]
- **CCC 3-1-T-1**: In addition to subcontrols in the ECC control 3-1-3, the CST shall cover the following additional subcontrols for cybersecurity requirements for cybersecurity resilience aspects of business continuity management, as a minimum: [applies to CST]
  - **CCC 3-1-T-1-1**: Developing and implementing disaster recovery and business continuity procedures related to cloud computing, in a secure manner. [applies to CST]

## CCC domain 4: Third-Party Cybersecurity

### CCC 4-1 Supply Chain and Third-Party Cybersecurity

Objective: To ensure the protection of assets against the cybersecurity risks related to thirdparties including outsourcing and managed services as per policies and procedures, and related laws and regulations.

- **CCC 4-1-P-1**: In addition to implementing the ECC controls 4-1-2 and 4-1-3, the CSP shall cover the following additional subcontrols for third-party cybersecurity requirements, as a minimum: [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 4-1-P-1-1**: Ensure that the CSP fulfills NCA's requests to remove software or services, provided by third-party providers that may be considered a cybersecurity threat to national entities, from the marketplace provided to CSTs. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 4-1-P-1-2**: Requirement to provide security documentation for any equipment or services from suppliers and third-party providers. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 4-1-P-1-3**: Third-party providers compliant with law and regulatory requirements relevant to their scope. [applies to CSP]
  - **CCC 4-1-P-1-4**: Risk management and security governance on third-party providers as part of general cybersecurity risk management and governance. [applies to CSP]
