# Operational Technology Cybersecurity Controls (OTCC-1:2022)

Publisher: National Cybersecurity Authority (NCA), Saudi Arabia. Source file: otcc_en.pdf.

Applicability is stated per facility level L1 (high criticality) to L3 (low).

## OTCC domain 1: Cybersecurity Governance

### OTCC 1-1 Cybersecurity Policies and Procedures

Objective: To ensure that OT/ICS cybersecurity requirements are documented, and complied with by the organization as per related laws and regulations, nizational requirements.

- **OTCC 1-1-1**: With reference to the ECC controls 1-3-1 and 1-3-2, the organization must document, approve, and implement a customized set of cybersecurity policies and procedures for OT/ICS systems or assets. [applicability: L1, L2, L3]
- **OTCC 1-1-2**: With reference to the ECC control 1-3-3, the cybersecurity OT/ ICS policies and procedures must be supported by cybersecurity requirements such as vendor recommendations, implementation guidelines, and configuration management guidelines. [applicability: L1, L2, L3]
- **OTCC 1-1-3**: With reference to the ECC control 1-3-4, OT/ICS cybersecurity policies and procedures must be reviewed periodically and/ or when there is a change in the risks landscape, organizational structure, and/or process changes. [applicability: L1, L2, L3]

### OTCC 1-2 Cybersecurity Roles and Responsibilities

Objective: To ensure that roles and responsibilities are defined for all parties implementing the operational technology cybersecurity controls organization.

- **OTCC 1-2-1**: In addition to the ECC subdomain 1-4, cybersecurity requir ments for Cybersecurity Roles and Responsibilities in OT/ICS must include, at a minimum, the following:
  - **OTCC 1-2-1-1**: Cybersecurity roles and responsibilities (RACI) assignment for all stakeholders of the OT/ICS assets must be defined, documented, communicated and approved by the Authorizing Official while ensuring there is no conflict of interest. [applicability: L1, L2, L3]
  - **OTCC 1-2-1-2**: Cybersecurity roles and responsibilities related to OT/ ICS assets must be assigned to the cybersecurity function in the organization. [applicability: L1, L2]

### OTCC 1-3 Cybersecurity Risk Management

Objective: To ensure managing cybersecurity risks in a methodological approach protect the organization’s OT/ICS assets as per organizational policies and related laws and regulations.

- **OTCC 1-3-1**: In addition to the ECC subdomain 1-5, cybersecurity requirements for cybersecurity risk management in OT/ICS must include, at a minimum, the following:
  - **OTCC 1-3-1-1**: OT/ICS cybersecurity risk management methodology must be included as part of the organization’s risk management and safety risk management methodologies. [applicability: L1, L2, L3]
  - **OTCC 1-3-1-2**: Cybersecurity risk assessment for OT/ICS assets must be conducted periodically while ensuring to include risks associated with signing contracts and agreements with OT/ICS related third-party organizations and/or upon changes in related regulatory requirements as part of the assessment. [applicability: L1, L2, L3]
  - **OTCC 1-3-1-3**: Risk register for OT/ICS cybersecurity risks must be included as part of the organization’s risk register. [applicability: L1, L2, L3]
  - **OTCC 1-3-1-4**: Appropriate level assignment to facilities which include (OT/ICS) must be conducted based on approved methodology. [applicability: L1, L2, L3]
  - **OTCC 1-3-1-5**: Include a qualitative analysis of cybersecurity risks within the Process Hazard Analysis (PHA) which is applied with any change in operations and/or procedures in Plants. [applicability: L1]
  - **OTCC 1-3-1-6**: In the event that cybersecurity requirements cannot be implemented within the OT/ICS environment, the specific justifications for not applying those requirements must be documented and approved by the respective cybersecurity function and the Authorizing Official. [applicability: L1, L2, L3]
  - **OTCC 1-3-1-7**: In the event of risk acceptance, alternative cybersecurity controls must be clearly defined, documented, approved by the Authorizing Official, and implemented effectively for a definedperiod of time while reassessing the risk continuously. [applicability: L1, L2, L3]

### OTCC 1-4 Cybersecurity in Industrial Control System Project Management

Objective: To ensure that cybersecurity requirements are included in project odology and procedures in order to maintain safe operations, confidentiality, and availability of OT/ICS assets as per organization policies and related laws and regulations.

- **OTCC 1-4-1**: In addition to the ECC controls 1-6-2 and 1-6-3, cybersecurity requirements in OT/ICS project management must include, at minimum, the following:
  - **OTCC 1-4-1-1**: Cybersecurity requirements must be part of OT/ICS project’s lifecycle. [applicability: L1, L2, L3]
  - **OTCC 1-4-1-2**: Cybersecurity requirements must be included as part of any functional and acceptance testing and evaluation process (such as Factory Acceptance Testing “FAT” , Site Acceptance Testing “SAT”, Commissioning Testing, Change Testing, Integration Testing and Source Code Review). [applicability: L1, L2]
  - **OTCC 1-4-1-3**: Secure-by-design principles must be included as part of security architectural designs for OT/ICS environments. [applicability: L1, L2, L3]
  - **OTCC 1-4-1-4**: System development environments including testing environment and integration platforms must be protected. [applicability: L1, L2]
- **OTCC 1-4-2**: Cybersecurity requirements within the organization’s OT/ICS project management must be reviewed, and their implementation effectiveness is measured and evaluated periodically. [applicability: L1, L2]

### OTCC 1-5 Cybersecurity in Change Management

Objective: To ensure that cybersecurity requirements are included in change odology and procedures in order to maintain safe implementation in OT/ICS environment by exercising due diligence analysis and es.

- **OTCC 1-5-1**: Cybersecurity requirements within the organization’s OT/ICS change management must be defined, documented, and approved. The cybersecurity requirements must be a key part of the overall requirements of OT/ICS change management. [applicability: L1, L2, L3]
- **OTCC 1-5-2**: Cybersecurity requirements within the organization’s OT/ICS change management lifecycle must be implemented. [applicability: L1, L2, L3]
- **OTCC 1-5-3**: In addition to the ECC controls 1-6-2 and 1-6-3, cybersecurity requirements in OT/ICS change management must include, at a minimum, the following:
  - **OTCC 1-5-3-1**: Cybersecurity requirements are part of the change management lifecycle. [applicability: L1, L2, L3]
  - **OTCC 1-5-3-2**: Changes are validated in a separate environment prior to implementing the changes on the production environment. [applicability: L1, L2]
  - **OTCC 1-5-3-3**: In the event that OT/ICS devices are replaced with different, but functionally equivalent devices, whether in design, testing, or operation environments, the cybersecurity of the replacement device must be validated prior to being utilized in operational environment. [applicability: L1, L2]
  - **OTCC 1-5-3-4**: Restricted processes for exceptional changes must be implemented. [applicability: L1, L2, L3]
  - **OTCC 1-5-3-5**: Automated configuration and asset change detection mechanisms must be implemented. [applicability: L1, L2]
- **OTCC 1-5-4**: Cybersecurity requirements within the organization’s OT/ICS change management requirements must be reviewed, and their implementation effectiveness is measured and evaluated periodically. [applicability: L1, L2]

### OTCC 1-6 Periodical Cybersecurity Review and Audit

Objective: To ensure that OT/ICS cybersecurity controls are implemented and with organizational policies and procedures, as well as related national tional laws, regulations and agreements.

- **OTCC 1-6-1**: With reference to ECC control 1-8-1, the organization’s cybersecurity function must review the implementation of (OTCC-1:2022) controls at least annually. [applicability: L1, L2, L3]
- **OTCC 1-6-2**: With reference to ECC control 1-8-2, the implementation of (OTCC-1:2022) controls must be reviewed by independent parties within the organization, outside the cybersecurity function at least once every three years. [applicability: L1, L2, L3]

### OTCC 1-7 Cybersecurity in Human Resources

Objective: To ensure that cybersecurity risks and requirements related to (employees and third party personnel) are managed efficiently prior during employment, after termination/separation as per organizational procedures, and related laws and regulations.

- **OTCC 1-7-1**: In addition to subcontrols in the ECC control 1-9-3, cybersecurity requirements related to human resources for OT/ICS environment must include, at a minimum, screening or vetting of all personnel (including employees, contractors and subcontractors) who have access or can utilize OT/ICS assets prior to granting them access. [applicability: L1, L2]
- **OTCC 1-7-2**: With reference to the ECC control 1-9-6, the cybersecurity requirements for cybersecurity in human resources in OT/ICS must be reviewed, and their implementation effectiveness is measured and evaluated periodically. [applicability: L1, L2]

### OTCC 1-8 Cybersecurity Awareness and Training Program

Objective: To ensure that personnel are aware of their cybersecurity responsibilities required cybersecurity awareness. It is also to ensure that personnel the required cybersecurity training, skills, and credentials needed cybersecurity responsibilities and to protect the organization’s OT/ICS

- **OTCC 1-8-1**: In addition to subcontrols in the ECC control 1-10-3, the cybers curity awareness program must include a secure and safe interaction with the OT/ICS assets or systems. [applicability: L1, L2, L3]
- **OTCC 1-8-2**: In addition to subcontrols in the ECC control 1-10-4, cybersecurity requirements in OT/ICS cybersecurity awareness and training program must include, at a minimum, the following:
  - **OTCC 1-8-2-1**: customized training, qualifications, knowledge, and professional skillsets must be provided to all personnel with access to the OT/ICS assets. The organization is encouraged to utilize the reference material provided in the Saudi Cybersecurity Workforce Framework (SCyWF). [applicability: L1, L2]
  - **OTCC 1-8-2-2**: Participation in OT/ICS authorized and/or specialized organizations and groups must be encouraged to stay up-to-date on common cybersecurity practices. [applicability: L1, L2]

## OTCC domain 2: Cybersecurity Defense

### OTCC 2-1 Asset Management

Objective: To ensure that the organization has an accurate and detailed inventory assets in order to support the organization’s cybersecurity and operational ments to maintain the production uptime, safe operations, confidentiality, and availability of OT/ICS assets.

- **OTCC 2-1-1**: In addition to the controls in ECC subdomain 2-1, cybersecurity requirements for asset management in OT/ICS environment must include, at a minimum, the following:
  - **OTCC 2-1-1-1**: OT/ICS assets inventory must be developed in electronic format for all OT/ICS assets, and reviewed periodically. [applicability: L1, L2, L3]
  - **OTCC 2-1-1-2**: Automated solution to collect asset inventory information must be utilized. [applicability: L1]
  - **OTCC 2-1-1-3**: OT/ICS asset inventory must be stored securely. [applicability: L1]
  - **OTCC 2-1-1-4**: Asset owners for all OT/ICS assets must be identified and involved throughout the relevant asset inventory management lifecycle. [applicability: L1, L2]
  - **OTCC 2-1-1-5**: Criticality rating for all assets must be assigned, documented, and approved by asset owners. [applicability: L1, L2]
- **OTCC 2-1-2**: With reference to the ECC control 2-1-6, the cybersecurity requirements for managing OT/ICS assets must be reviewed, and their implementation effectiveness is measured and evaluated periodically. [applicability: L1, L2]

### OTCC 2-2 Identity and Access Management

Objective: To ensure secure and restricted logical access to OT/ICS assets in unauthorized access and allow only authorized access for users, which to accomplish assigned tasks.

- **OTCC 2-2-1**: In addition to subcontrols in ECC control 2-2-3, cybersecurity requirements for identity and access management in OT/ICS environment must include, at a minimum, the following:
  - **OTCC 2-2-1-1**: Identity and access management lifecycle for OT/ICS is separated and independent from Information Technology (IT) including centrally managed identity and access management solutions. [applicability: L1]
  - **OTCC 2-2-1-2**: Service accounts must be managed securely for OT/ICS services, applications, systems, and devices that are separated and disconnected from interactive users account logins. [applicability: L1, L2]
  - **OTCC 2-2-1-3**: Default credentials for all OT/ICS assets must be changed, disabled, or removed. [applicability: L1, L2, L3]
  - **OTCC 2-2-1-4**: Sessions must be managed securely, including session authenticity, session lockout, and session timeout termination. [applicability: L1]
  - **OTCC 2-2-1-5**: Automatic disabling/removing of service accounts, programs, or accounts related to (OT/ICS) assets must be prevented, except for monitoring systems. [applicability: L1]
  - **OTCC 2-2-1-6**: Dual approval and explicit privilege escalation mechanisms for sensitive actions within the OT/ICS environment must be employed. [applicability: L1]
  - **OTCC 2-2-1-7**: Remote access to the OT/ICS networks must be restricted and exceptionally enabled when necessary and justified. A cybersecurity risk assessment must be conducted prior to granting remote access and its associated risks are monitored and managed. The granted access must be through trusted multi-factor authenticated and encrypted channel for a defined period of time and with limited access privilege. The remote access session must be monitored and recorded while its time duration and granted user's privilege must be in accordance with the cybersecurity risk assessment. [applicability: L1, L2]
  - **OTCC 2-2-1-8**: Secure and complex password standards must be implemented. [applicability: L1, L2]
  - **OTCC 2-2-1-9**: Secure mechanisms to store OT/ICS assets’ passwords must be used. [applicability: L1]
  - **OTCC 2-2-1-10**: With reference to the ECC subcontrol 2-2-3-5, users’ identities and access rights must be reviewed in response to cybersecurity incidents, personnel roles changes, or whenever there is a change in OT/ICS system architecture. [applicability: L1, L2]
  - **OTCC 2-2-1-11**: Access shall be immediately revoked when no longer needed. [applicability: L1, L2]
- **OTCC 2-2-2**: With reference to the ECC control 2-2-4, the cybersecurity requirements for identity and access management in OT/ICS env ronment must be reviewed, and its implementation effectiveness is measured and evaluated periodically. [applicability: L1, L2]

### OTCC 2-3 System and Processing Facilities Protection

Objective: To ensure the protection of OT/ICS systems and processing facilities workstations, servers and Safety Instrumented Systems “SIS”) against

- **OTCC 2-3-1**: In addition to subcontrols in the ECC control 2-3-3, cybersecurity requirements for system and processing facility protection in OT/ ICS environment must include, at a minimum, the following:
  - **OTCC 2-3-1-1**: Advanced, up-to-date protection mechanisms and techniques must be utilized and securely managed to block and protect from malware, Advanced Persistent Threats (APT), malicious files, and activities. [applicability: L1, L2]
  - **OTCC 2-3-1-2**: Periodic security configurations’ review and hardening must be conducted in alignment with the vendor implementation guidance or recommendations with respect to cybersecurity and organization’s formal change management mechanisms. [applicability: L1]
  - **OTCC 2-3-1-3**: Periodic security patches and upgrades must be implemented in alignment with vendor implementation guidance or recommendations with respect to cybersecurity and organization’s formal change management mechanisms. [applicability: L1]
  - **OTCC 2-3-1-4**: Principles of least privilege and least functionality must be applied. [applicability: L1, L2]
  - **OTCC 2-3-1-5**: Safety Instrumented Systems (SIS) controllers must be configured in appropriate modes at all times, which prevent any unauthorized changes, and changes to improper modes are limited to exceptional cases with a specific period of time. [applicability: L1, L2]
  - **OTCC 2-3-1-6**: Application whitelisting techniques or other similar techniques must be deployed to limit the applications that are allowed to run in OT/ICS environment. [applicability: L1, L2]
  - **OTCC 2-3-1-7**: OT/ICS assets must be managed through dedicated, segmented and hardened Engineering Workstation (EWS) and Human-Machine Interface (HMI) for management purposes and maintenance. [applicability: L1]
  - **OTCC 2-3-1-8**: External storage media is scanned and analyzed against malware and APT. The scan must be executed in an isolated and secure environment. [applicability: L1, L2]
  - **OTCC 2-3-1-9**: Usage of external storage media in the production environment must be restricted unless secure mechanisms for data transfer are developed and properly implemented. [applicability: L1, L2, L3]
  - **OTCC 2-3-1-10**: Systems’ logs and critical files must be protected from unauthorized access, tampering, illegitimate modification and/or deletion. [applicability: L1]
  - **OTCC 2-3-1-11**: Unauthorized applications, scripts, tasks, and changes must be detected and analyzed. [applicability: L1, L2]
  - **OTCC 2-3-1-12**: New communications sessions and commands execution must be detected and analyzed. [applicability: L1, L2]
  - **OTCC 2-3-1-13**: Direct communications between the OT/ICS environment and external hosts must be detected and analyzed. [applicability: L1, L2, L3]
- **OTCC 2-3-2**: With reference to the ECC control 2-3-4, the cybersecurity requirements for system and processing facilities protection in OT/ ICS environment must be reviewed, and their implementation effectiveness is measured and evaluated periodically. [applicability: L1, L2]

### OTCC 2-4 Networks Security Management

Objective: To ensure the protection of the organization’s OT/ICS networks from

- **OTCC 2-4-1**: In addition to subcontrols in ECC control 2-5-3, cybersecurity requirements for network security management in OT/ICS environment must cover, at a minimum, the following:
  - **OTCC 2-4-1-1**: OT/ICS environment must be segmented logically or physically from other environments or networks. [applicability: L1, L2, L3]
  - **OTCC 2-4-1-2**: Different zones within the OT/ICS environment must be segmented logically or physically in accordance with the zone’s appropriate level that isolates data flows and directs traffic to "Choke Points”. [applicability: L1]
  - **OTCC 2-4-1-3**: Safety Instrumented Systems (SIS) must be segmented logically or physically from other OT/ICS networks. [applicability: L1, L2, L3]
  - **OTCC 2-4-1-4**: Wireless technologies (such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, cellular, satellite, etc.) must be restricted, and to only be used when the technology meets specific business requirements and is properly secured. [applicability: L1, L2]
  - **OTCC 2-4-1-5**: Wireless technologies must be segmented logically or physically from other OT/ICS networks. [applicability: L1, L2]
  - **OTCC 2-4-1-6**: Network communications, services, and connection points between different zones must be limited to the minimum to meet operational, maintenance, and safety requirements. [applicability: L1, L2, L3]
  - **OTCC 2-4-1-7**: Direct exposure of common remote authentication and access management services on external-facing hosts must be prevented. [applicability: L1, L2, L3]
  - **OTCC 2-4-1-8**: Only authorized business-critical services are accessible from the internal OT/ICS networks, and accessibility to services with known vulnerabilities must be limited to the greatest extent possible. [applicability: L1, L2, L3]
  - **OTCC 2-4-1-9**: Direct communications between corporate zone and OT/ ICS zones must be prevented, and direct all the required connections through dedicated, secured, and hardened jump host/solution in the DMZ zone. [applicability: L1, L2]
  - **OTCC 2-4-1-10**: Remote access point in the DMZ zone must not be connected to the OT/ICS networks unless needed, while ensuring that the session is multi-factor authenticated, recorded, and established for a defined period of time only. [applicability: L1, L2]
  - **OTCC 2-4-1-11**: Proxies must be employed between the corporate and OT/ICS zones for all machine-to-machine traffic. [applicability: L1, L2]
  - **OTCC 2-4-1-12**: Dedicated gateways must be used to segment OT/ICS networks from corporate zone. [applicability: L1]
  - **OTCC 2-4-1-13**: Dedicated DMZ zone must be used to reside any system that needs services provided by corporate zone. [applicability: L1, L2, L3]
  - **OTCC 2-4-1-14**: Strict limitation on enabling/usage of industrial protocols and ports to the minimum to meet operational, maintenance, and safety requirements. [applicability: L1, L2, L3]
  - **OTCC 2-4-1-15**: Periodic patches and upgrades for production assets must be certified by respective vendor and tested in a separate environment prior to implementation. [applicability: L1, L2]
  - **OTCC 2-4-1-16**: Details related to network architecture and topology, zones, network data flows, connectivity, and interdependencies must be documented, updated, and maintained. [applicability: L1, L2, L3]
- **OTCC 2-4-2**: With reference to the ECC control 2-5-4, the cybersecurity requirements for network security management in OT/ICS environment must be reviewed, and their implementation effectiveness measured and evaluated periodically. [applicability: L1, L2]

### OTCC 2-5 Mobile Devices Security

Objective: To ensure the protection of mobile devices (including laptops, handheld devices, network test devices, etc.) from cyber risks and to ensure of sensitive data and the organization’s information.

- **OTCC 2-5-1**: In addition to subcontrols in the ECC control 2-6-3, cybersecurity requirements for mobile device security in OT/ICS must cover, at a minimum, the following:
  - **OTCC 2-5-1-1**: Usage of mobile devices for OT/ICS must be restricted unless specifically required. A cybersecurity risk assessment must be conducted where risks must be defined and managed. A management approval must be granted by respective cybersecurity function for a defined period of time only in alignment with organization’s formal access management mechanisms. [applicability: L1, L2]
  - **OTCC 2-5-1-2**: Mobile devices must only be used for their intended purposes and in compliance with cybersecurity requirements of its respective zones prior to being connected to OT/ICS environment, and are hardened and updated with the latest security patches and scanned against malware and APT. [applicability: L1, L2]
  - **OTCC 2-5-1-3**: Limited and approved list of mobile devices must be defined while ensuring that only these mobile devices can be connected to OT/ICS environment. [applicability: L1, L2]
  - **OTCC 2-5-1-4**: Centralized management of mobile devices must be deployed. [applicability: L1]
  - **OTCC 2-5-1-5**: Encryptions mechanisms must be used for mobile devices authorized to access the OT/ICS assets. [applicability: L1]
- **OTCC 2-5-2**: With reference to the ECC control 2-6-4, the cybersecurity requirements for mobile devices security in OT/ICS environment must be reviewed, and their implementation effectiveness is measured and evaluated periodically. [applicability: L1, L2]

### OTCC 2-6 Data and Information Protection

Objective: To ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of organization’s and information as per organizational policies and procedures, and laws and regulations.

- **OTCC 2-6-1**: In addition to subcontrols in the ECC control 2-7-3, cybersecurity requirements for data and information protection in OT/ICS must include, at a minimum, the following:
  - **OTCC 2-6-1-1**: Electronic and physical data (at rest and in transit) must be protected at a level consistent with its classification. [applicability: L1, L2, L3]
  - **OTCC 2-6-1-2**: Data Leakage Prevention (DLP) mechanisms must be used to protect the classified data and information. [applicability: L1, L2]
  - **OTCC 2-6-1-3**: Secure wiping mechanisms for configuration details and stored data from OT/ICS assets prior to decommissioning must be implemented. [applicability: L1, L2]
  - **OTCC 2-6-1-4**: Transfer or usage of OT systems’ data in any environment other than production environment must be limited, except after applying strict controls for protecting that data. [applicability: L1]
- **OTCC 2-6-2**: With reference to the ECC control 2-7-4, the cybersecurity requirements for data and information protection in OT/ICS environment must be reviewed, and their implementation effectiveness is measured and evaluated periodically. [applicability: L1, L2]

### OTCC 2-11 Cybersecurity Event Logs and Monitoring Management

Objective: To ensure timely collection, analysis, and monitoring of cybersecurity detection of potential cyber-attacks in order to prevent or minimize pacts on the organization’s operations.

- **OTCC 2-11-1**: In addition to subcontrols in the ECC control 2-12-3, cybersecurity requirements for cybersecurity event logs and monitoring management in OT/ICS must include, at a minimum, the following:
  - **OTCC 2-11-1-1**: Cybersecurity event logs and audit trails must be activated for all OT/ICS assets. [applicability: L1, L2, L3]
  - **OTCC 2-11-1-2**: Failure attempts in accessing the organization’s monitoring systems must be detected and logged. [applicability: L1, L2, L3]
  - **OTCC 2-11-1-3**: Continuous, in-depth cybersecurity log review and monitoring, covering all logs and audit trails must be conducted. [applicability: L1]
  - **OTCC 2-11-1-4**: Monitoring, detecting, and analyzing User Behaviors Analytics (UBA) must be performed. [applicability: L1]
  - **OTCC 2-11-1-5**: Upload or download activities of OT/ICS assets including Safety Instrumented Systems (SIS) must be detected. [applicability: L1]
  - **OTCC 2-11-1-6**: All remote access sessions must be monitored. [applicability: L1, L2]
